# Additional product-scene source research

Checked October 30, 2026. **No new training examples are admitted by this
research.** Two genuine product-oriented editable works were identified, plus
one useful blank advertising layout. None yet meets the complete requirement
of an independently sourced product advertisement/poster with verified original
asset rights and a faithful portable import. This is a bounded search result,
not a claim that suitable material does not exist.

The existing 18 designs, ten reserves and observed 34-image pilot remain
unchanged. No new import, resize, label, optimizer run or model promotion was
performed. Downloaded research evidence lives only under ignored
`output/scene-product-research/`.

## Concrete new leads

### 1. WakaFlower promotional hero — Wakasso

This is an authored **commerce landing-page hero**, not a print poster: a large
bouquet supports sales copy, an ordering action and two priced plant cards.
Its native reference was visually inspected. The ground-truth JSON contains
48 editable units, including 20 text units, original text strings, Figma styles,
transforms and image-fill references. It therefore offers a useful physical
product-versus-background example outside Solstice; the reference PNG is only
evidence of its appearance, not the editable input.

- Original-author source: [Figma Community file 1196765971875947186](https://www.figma.com/community/file/1196765971875947186).
- Exact published ground truth: [episode 1196765971875947186_3_1227](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Jintae-Park/ReDesign-Figma909/resolve/74ed7b1fca5b005167ed21c18fdef186cd18d2f2/valid_frames/1196765971875947186_3_1227.json).
- JSON SHA-256: `3ae50334ac014b72b00652241fd9d0e140a071bfa8e338b977011a3a4ec46708`.
- Native reference: [original Figma frame render](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Jintae-Park/ReDesign-Figma909/resolve/74ed7b1fca5b005167ed21c18fdef186cd18d2f2/unit_images/XYyub8zzMdMjaySX0aIgpc_3_1227/_original_3_1227.png),
  SHA-256 `86a73ab671a7596ac6723d23a924ce46687a62d327a1304fcba051f2fa783fa4`.

**Rights and readiness:** the benchmark publisher records Wakasso and CC BY 4.0
for this original Community work. That is a specific attribution claim, not a
license inferred from the ReDesign code. However, the original Figma page could
not be fetched in this session. The inspected record has image references but
no photographer, stock-source URL or separate photo-license ledger. Obtain that
ledger or original-author confirmation covering the included bouquet/plant
images before admitting them. Exact Playfair Display, Montserrat and Pacifico
faces, their notices, and original per-layer images are also required; none was
downloaded or substituted here. A new Figma-ground-truth adapter and source
parity check would be necessary. This candidate is **held**, not approved.

### 2. Remote Voice Control concept poster — Soham Ningurkar

gradients and effects. No `IMAGE` fill occurs in the inspected JSON, reducing
the separate stock-photo dependency. Its two control variants are one authored
work and one source family, not two independent examples.

### 3. Benefit-stack advertisement layout — Bytown / Tomas Kolafa

Bytown publishes a native editable SVG for a product-benefit advertisement,
with separate text and shapes and a photo placeholder. Its FAQ permits using
the templates in one's own business ads without attribution and prohibits
reselling the pack. This is a first-party use grant, not an open dataset
redistribution license. The linked live toothpaste ad is a separate reference,
not a licensed editable template or a grant to reuse its product photography.
[Publisher page and FAQ](https://bytown.co/ad-examples/ai-ad-templates),
[exact benefit-stack SVG](https://bytown.co/ad-examples/ai-ad-templates/benefit-stack.svg).

**Held:** obtain explicit redistribution/training terms and an actual
rights-cleared product asset. Filling its placeholder ourselves would create
a newly authored composition, not discover an existing independent product ad.
No SVG/photo pack was downloaded, imported or used for training here.

## Sources deliberately not treated as cleared product data

- **Crello:** its card includes editable text/font/geometry fields and advertises
  CDLA-Permissive-2.0, but its own licensing section retains VistaCreate terms,
  disclaims ownership of original template copyrights and says original files
  cannot be redistributed. Do not infer blanket original-asset clearance from
  the dataset badge. Obtain an applicable grant for the intended data use and
  exact assets first. [Official card, licensing section](https://huggingface.co/datasets/cyberagent/crello/blob/cc8ab9d272c3f215959eaa40b10f255b80d04bbf/README.md),
  [publisher's referenced VistaCreate terms](https://create.vista.com/faq/legal/licensing/license_agreements/).
- **PosterSVG:** its one editable demonstration is a model reconstruction from
  a poster image, and its public dataset/license remain announced rather than
  released. The repository's MIT code license establishes neither original
  poster authorship nor product-asset rights. It is outside this request for
  author-native originals. [Official README](https://github.com/csust-sonie/PosterSVG).
- **Visual-studio-skill:** its product campaign examples explicitly withhold
  reuse, redistribution, model training and commercial derivatives. Public
  visibility does not overcome those exclusions. [Publisher's example scope](https://github.com/Wang-Yeah623/Visual-studio-skill).

## Before a new experiment

First obtain two or three genuinely independent author-native product ads with
complete layout, photo/vector, font and icon rights. Freeze their original
bytes, source family and split **before** any optimization; keep them separate
from the already observed pilot. Verify editable-source parity with an
independent native reference, then label image roles and any measured product
regions with their actual review origin. Background proposals must still grant
no crop authority. Neither a blank template, a newly filled placeholder nor a
vector concept should silently become a commercial-product validation claim.

Research evidence: all 909 Figma frame **metadata prefixes** were inspected
for source/title/license leads, not all 909 full designs or assets. Seven
candidate JSON records plus two initial schema probes were read in full; only
the two product-oriented native references above were visually reviewed. The
saved prefix index SHA-256 is
`e2db167ee37e449e91b38d7442c94a44845f47592c5c1d699e782a1d26caee7b`.
There is no corpus-wide product-coverage or rights-approval claim.
